## 📅 **Three-Phase Deployment Timeline**

### **Phase XI-A: Shadow Mode Validation (Months 1-3)**

#### **Objectives**
- Validate real-world performance against simulation predictions
- Build regulatory confidence with zero-risk deployment
- Establish baseline operational metrics
- Train clinical teams on platform

#### **Study Design**
- **Sites:** 10 pilot sites (5 US, 3 EU, 2 Asia-Pacific)
- **Subjects:** 100 Parkinson's patients (10 per site)
- **Duration:** 8 weeks per subject
- **Mode:** Shadow only (no device actuation)

#### **Site Selection Criteria**
```
Tier 1 Sites (Academic Medical Centers):
├── Johns Hopkins (Baltimore, MD) - PI: Dr. Zoltan Mari
├── Stanford Medicine (Palo Alto, CA) - PI: Dr. Helen Bronte-Stewart
├── University College London (UK) - PI: Dr. Patricia Limousin
├── Charité Berlin (Germany) - PI: Dr. Andrea Kühn
└── University of Sydney (Australia) - PI: Dr. Simon Lewis

Tier 2 Sites (Regional Centers):
├── Cleveland Clinic (Ohio) - Movement Disorders Center
├── Emory University (Atlanta) - Neurology Department
├── University of Toronto (Canada) - Krembil Brain Institute
├── Radboud University (Netherlands) - Donders Institute
└── University of Melbourne (Australia) - Florey Institute
```

#### **Enrollment Criteria**
**Inclusion:**
- Idiopathic Parkinson's disease diagnosis (Hoehn & Yahr 2-3)
- Age 50-80 years
- Stable medication regimen ≥4 weeks
- Adequate cognitive function (MoCA ≥24)
- Capable of independent EEG setup with minimal assistance

**Exclusion:**
- Deep brain stimulation (DBS) implants
- Significant cognitive impairment
- Unstable medical conditions
- Inability to comply with study procedures

#### **Primary Endpoints**
- **Decision Accuracy:** AI decisions match expert clinical judgment ≥95%
- **System Reliability:** Uptime ≥99.5%, zero critical failures
- **Data Quality:** Complete audit trail integrity 100%
- **Safety:** Zero adverse events related to system malfunction

#### **Key Activities**
```
Month 1: Site Initiation
├── IRB/Ethics approvals at all sites
├── Site training and certification
├── Technology deployment and testing
└── First subject enrollment

Month 2: Full Enrollment
├── Parallel enrollment across all sites
├── Weekly safety monitoring calls
├── Real-time data quality monitoring
└── Interim safety analysis (50% enrollment)

Month 3: Data Analysis
├── Complete follow-up visits
├── Comprehensive data analysis
├── Regulatory submission preparation
└── Phase XI-B protocol finalization
```

### **Phase XI-B: Assist Mode Trials (Months 4-9)**

#### **Objectives**
- Validate human-AI collaboration in clinical practice
- Demonstrate safety of AI-guided interventions
- Measure clinician acceptance and workflow integration
- Generate preliminary efficacy signals

#### **Study Design**
- **Sites:** Expand to 15 sites (add 5 high-volume centers)
- **Subjects:** 200 patients (randomized 1:1 AI-assist vs standard care)
- **Duration:** 12 weeks intervention + 4 weeks follow-up
- **Mode:** Assist (AI recommendations with clinician approval)

#### **Additional Sites**
```
Expansion Sites:
├── Mayo Clinic (Rochester, MN) - Dr. J. Eric Ahlskog
├── UCSF (San Francisco, CA) - Dr. Jill Ostrem
├── Mount Sinai (New York, NY) - Dr. Michele Tagliati
├── University of Edinburgh (UK) - Dr. Richard Walker
└── McGill University (Montreal, CA) - Dr. Anne-Louise Lafontaine
```

#### **Randomization Schema**
```
Stratified Block Randomization:
├── Site (15 sites)
├── Disease severity (H&Y 2 vs 3)
├── Age group (<65 vs ≥65)
└── Gender (male vs female)

Allocation: 1:1
├── AI-Assist Group (n=100)
│   ├── Adaptive neurofeedback with AI recommendations
│   ├── Clinician approval required for all adjustments
│   └── Standard care + technology platform
└── Control Group (n=100)
    ├── Standard neurofeedback protocols
    ├── Clinician-determined parameters
    └── Sham AI interface (shadow logging only)
```

#### **Primary Endpoints**
- **Clinician Agreement:** ≥85% acceptance rate of AI recommendations
- **Safety:** Non-inferiority to standard care (safety margin ≤5%)
- **Workflow Integration:** ≤10% increase in session duration
- **Patient Satisfaction:** ≥80% prefer AI-assisted sessions

#### **Secondary Endpoints**
- **Motor Function:** UPDRS-III change from baseline
- **Quality of Life:** PDQ-39 improvement
- **Treatment Adherence:** Session completion rates
- **System Performance:** Real-time safety gate performance

#### **DSMB Oversight**
```
Data Safety Monitoring Board:
├── Chair: Dr. Michael Okun (University of Florida)
├── Biostatistician: Dr. Charity Morgan (University of Alabama)
├── Neurologist: Dr. Alberto Espay (University of Cincinnati)
├── Bioethicist: Dr. Arthur Kaplan (NYU Langone)
└── Patient Advocate: Brian Grant (Brian Grant Foundation)

Review Schedule:
├── Month 5: 25% enrollment safety review
├── Month 6: 50% enrollment interim analysis
├── Month 7: 75% enrollment safety review
└── Month 8: Final safety analysis and Phase XI-C recommendation
```

### **Phase XI-C: Active Mode Pivotal RCT (Months 10-18)**

#### **Objectives**
- Demonstrate superior efficacy vs standard neurofeedback
- Confirm safety of fully autonomous adaptive AI
- Generate pivotal evidence for FDA submission
- Establish health economic value proposition

#### **Study Design**
- **Sites:** 25 sites globally (15 US, 10 international)
- **Subjects:** 400 patients (powered for 15% effect size)
- **Duration:** 16 weeks intervention + 8 weeks follow-up
- **Mode:** Active (fully autonomous adaptive AI)

#### **Global Site Network**
```
US Sites (n=15): Existing 10 + 5 new high-volume centers
International Sites (n=10):
├── Toronto Western Hospital (Canada)
├── University of Oxford (UK)
├── Karolinska Institute (Sweden)
├── University Medical Center Utrecht (Netherlands)
├── Pitié-Salpêtrière Hospital (France)
├── Universität Tübingen (Germany)
├── University of Melbourne (Australia)
├── National University Hospital Singapore
├── Seoul National University Hospital (South Korea)
└── University of São Paulo (Brazil)
```

#### **Primary Efficacy Endpoint**
**Motor Function Improvement:** Change in UPDRS-III total score from baseline to week 16

**Power Calculation:**
- Effect size: 15% improvement (6 points on UPDRS-III)
- Power: 90%
- Alpha: 0.05 (two-sided)
- Dropout rate: 15%
- Sample size: 400 patients (200 per arm)

#### **Secondary Endpoints**
```
Efficacy Endpoints:
├── UPDRS-II (Activities of Daily Living)
├── PDQ-39 (Quality of Life)
├── Timed Up and Go Test
├── 9-Hole Peg Test
├── Patient Global Impression of Change
└── Caregiver burden assessment

Safety Endpoints:
├── Adverse events (all grades)
├── Serious adverse events
├── Device-related adverse events
├── Study discontinuation rates
└── Neurological safety assessments

Biomarker Endpoints:
├── EEG biomarker changes
├── Motor cortex plasticity measures
├── Sleep quality improvements
└── Cognitive function assessments
```

#### **Statistical Analysis Plan**
```
Primary Analysis:
├── Modified Intent-to-Treat (mITT) population
├── Mixed-effects model for repeated measures (MMRM)
├── Baseline UPDRS-III, site, and stratification factors as covariates
└── Missing data: Multiple imputation

Secondary Analyses:
├── Per-protocol population analysis
├── Responder analyses (≥3, ≥6, ≥9 point improvements)
├── Time-to-response analysis
├── Subgroup analyses (age, disease duration, severity)
└── Health economic evaluations
```

├── Mitigation: Continuous performance monitoring with automatic rollback

├── Mitigation: Redundant systems and local backup protocols
