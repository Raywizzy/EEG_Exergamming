
## 🏥 **IRB Template Package for Multi-Site Deployment**

### **Master IRB Template Structure**

**Protocol Title:** "Safety and Efficacy of AI-Adaptive EEG Neurofeedback for Parkinson's Disease Motor Symptoms: A Multi-Center, Randomized Controlled Trial"

**Study Design Overview:**
```
Study Type: Prospective, multi-center, randomized, sham-controlled
Phase: Phase XI Clinical Deployment (Shadow/Assist/Active progression)
Duration: 18 months (6 months per operational mode)
Sample Size: 300 participants across 10 sites (30 per site)
Randomization: 2:1 active:sham ratio with stratification by site and disease severity

Primary Endpoint: Change in UPDRS Part III motor score from baseline to 12 weeks
Secondary Endpoints:
├── Quality of life measures (PDQ-39, EQ-5D-5L)
├── Functional independence (Schwab & England ADL Scale)
├── Biomarker changes (alpha/beta power ratio, coherence measures)
└── Safety and tolerability (adverse events, dropout rates)
```

### **Informed Consent Template Components**

**Section 1: Study Purpose and Procedures**
```
Study Purpose:
"This research study is testing a new computer-based therapy system that uses
brain wave monitoring (EEG) to provide personalized feedback for improving
movement problems in Parkinson's disease. The system uses artificial intelligence
to automatically adjust the therapy based on your brain signals."

Procedure Description:
├── Screening visit with medical history and movement assessment
├── EEG setup with comfortable, non-invasive electrodes
├── Therapy sessions with real-time brain signal monitoring
├── Progressive system autonomy (shadow → assist → active modes)
├── Safety monitoring with immediate human supervision available
└── Follow-up assessments at 4, 8, 12, and 24 weeks
```

**Section 2: Risks and Benefits**
```
Potential Risks:
├── Minimal risk from EEG electrode placement (skin irritation)
├── Theoretical risk of headache or fatigue from neurofeedback
├── Privacy considerations for brain signal data collection
├── Potential emotional response to therapy feedback
└── Risk mitigation through comprehensive safety monitoring

Potential Benefits:
├── Possible improvement in motor symptoms and movement control
├── Potential reduction in therapy session duration and frequency
├── Access to innovative treatment not otherwise available
├── Contribution to advancing Parkinson's disease treatment
└── No guarantee of individual benefit, research nature emphasized
```

**Section 3: Privacy and Data Protection**
```
Data Collection and Use:
├── EEG signals, movement assessments, and therapy response data
├── Video recordings of therapy sessions for analysis (optional)
├── Medical history and medication information relevant to study
├── Quality of life questionnaires and functional assessments

Privacy Protection:
├── Data de-identification with unique study ID assignment
├── Secure data storage with encryption and access controls
├── Limited data sharing with regulatory authorities as required
├── Right to request data deletion upon study withdrawal
└── HIPAA compliance with additional research protections
```
