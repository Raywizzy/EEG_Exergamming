📋 Data Requirements

BIDS Format

Your EEG data must be organized according to the Brain Imaging Data Structure (BIDS) standard:

dataset/
├── sub-001/
│   └── eeg/
│       ├── sub-001_task-rest_eeg.bdf
│       └── sub-001_task-rest_eeg.json
├── sub-002/
│   └── eeg/
│       ├── sub-002_task-rest_eeg.bdf
│       └── sub-002_task-rest_eeg.json
└── dataset_description.json
Technical Requirements
  • File formats: .bdf, .edf, or .set (EEGLab)
  • Channels: Minimum 19 channels, 64+ recommended
  • Duration: At least 2 minutes of resting-state data
  • Sampling rate: 256 Hz or higher
  • File size: Maximum 1GB per ZIP upload
Channel Requirements

For optimal Core15+ performance, ensure these channels are present:

Motor: C3, Cz, C4
Posterior: P3, Pz, P4, O1, Oz, O2
Frontal: Fp1, Fp2, F3, Fz, F4

🔧 Troubleshooting

Common Issues
❌ "BIDS format validation failed"
Solution: Ensure your dataset follows BIDS structure with proper subject directories (sub-XXX/) and EEG files in the correct locations.
❌ "Insufficient channels detected"
Solution: Core15+ requires at least 19 EEG channels. Check that your data includes the required motor, posterior, and frontal channels.
❌ "Recording duration too short"
Solution: Ensure each subject has at least 2 minutes of continuous EEG data for reliable feature extraction.
⚠️ "High artifact ratio detected"
Solution: Review data quality. High artifact levels may affect biomarker performance. Consider re-recording or improved preprocessing.
Data Quality Tips
  • Use impedances below 10 kΩ during recording
  • Minimize eye blinks and muscle artifacts
  • Ensure stable electrode connections
  • Record in quiet, comfortable environment
  • Include sufficient resting-state periods
