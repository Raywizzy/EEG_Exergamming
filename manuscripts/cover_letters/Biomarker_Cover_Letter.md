# Cover Letter: Biomarker Validation Paper

**To**: The Editors, npj Parkinson's Disease
**Subject**: Submission of Manuscript: Multi-Site Validation of Core5 Beta-Burst Biomarkers for Parkinson's Disease Using CORAL Domain Adaptation

---

Dear Editors,

I am pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "Multi-Site Validation of Core5 Beta-Burst Biomarkers for Parkinson's Disease Using CORAL Domain Adaptation" for consideration in npj Parkinson's Disease.

This study provides the first external, multi-site validation of EEG biomarkers for Parkinson's disease. Using resting-state EEG from UCSD (ds002778) and Iowa (ds004584), we validated the locked Core5 biomarker panel with CORAL domain adaptation to address cross-site differences. Results from 127 participants (82 PD, 45 healthy controls) demonstrated mean balanced accuracy of 65.5%, exceeding the prespecified clinical threshold, with one site achieving 70.8%. Biomarker distributions were physiologically valid, and processing latency was <500 ms per subject, supporting scalability for clinical deployment.

We believe this manuscript is well suited to npj Parkinson's Disease because it delivers clinically interpretable, externally validated EEG biomarkers that directly address the unmet need for objective PD diagnostics and monitoring tools. The findings establish a regulatory-compliant foundation for prospective multi-site clinical trials and translation into practice.

This manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by any other journal. All authors have approved the submission.

Thank you for considering our work. We believe it will be of strong interest to your readership in Parkinson's disease, neurophysiology, and biomarker development.
