# Cover Letter: Infrastructure Innovation Paper

**To**: The Editors, npj Digital Medicine
**Subject**: Submission of Manuscript: A Gold-Standard Framework for Automated Multi-Site EEG Biomarker Validation with Real-Time Status Monitoring

---

Dear Editors,

I am pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "A Gold-Standard Framework for Automated Multi-Site EEG Biomarker Validation with Real-Time Status Monitoring" for consideration in npj Digital Medicine.

This work presents the first automated, self-monitoring validation platform for EEG biomarkers. The framework introduces three key innovations: (i) a real-time traffic-light badge system for quality control, (ii) auto-updating manuscripts and dashboards, and (iii) locked pipelines with audit trails aligned to regulatory standards. Demonstration on two independent Parkinson's disease EEG datasets achieved a mean balanced accuracy of 65.5%, exceeding the prespecified 65% clinical threshold, while reducing validation time by approximately 70% compared with traditional methods.

We believe this manuscript is well suited to npj Digital Medicine due to its focus on reproducibility, regulatory compliance, and digital infrastructure innovation. The work establishes a reference standard for clinical trial readiness and provides a plug-and-play architecture that can be adopted across neurotechnology and digital health research.

This manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by any other journal. All authors have approved the submission.

Thank you for considering our work for publication. We believe it will be of strong interest to your readership in digital health, regulatory science, and clinical translation.
