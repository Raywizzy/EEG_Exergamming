# Research Highlights

## For npj Digital Medicine Submission

### 5 Key Highlights (Required by Nature Portfolio)

1. **First self-monitoring EEG biomarker validation framework with real-time performance tracking and automated quality control systems**

2. **Regulatory-grade infrastructure compliant with FDA/EMA digital biomarker guidelines, featuring locked pipelines and comprehensive audit trails**

3. **Demonstrated scalability across 564 subjects from three independent datasets with automated cross-site harmonization via CORAL domain adaptation**

4. **Novel traffic-light badge system enabling immediate assessment of validation status and automated triggering of optimization alerts**

5. **Biomarker-agnostic architecture providing reusable reference framework for advancing any EEG-based digital health application toward clinical deployment**
