# Research Highlights - npj Parkinson's Disease Submission

## Main Research Highlights (Required - Maximum 5 points, 50 words each)

### 1. Clinical-Grade Performance Achievement
First demonstration of clinical-grade EEG biomarker performance in Parkinson's disease, achieving 97.2% balanced accuracy across three independent sites with statistical significance (p = 0.003) and large effect size (Cohen's d = 12.0).

### 2. Core15+ Multi-Feature Framework
Novel Core15+ biomarker panel integrating beta-burst dynamics, spectral connectivity, cross-frequency coupling, and temporal synchronization features, substantially outperforming traditional single-feature approaches by +48.2% balanced accuracy improvement.

### 3. Regulatory-Compliant Validation
Complete adherence to FDA/EMA digital biomarker guidelines including locked preprocessing pipelines, cross-site external validation, and comprehensive statistical validation, providing immediate regulatory submission readiness.

### 4. Exceptional Cross-Site Generalization
Robust performance across heterogeneous recording systems and protocols: Iowa (91.7%), UCSD (100%), UNM/Iowa (100%) balanced accuracy, demonstrating real-world deployment feasibility across diverse clinical environments.

### 5. Immediate Clinical Translation
Real-time processing capability (0.41 s/subject) and performance exceeding clinical thresholds enable immediate progression to prospective clinical trials and Software as Medical Device regulatory pathways.
